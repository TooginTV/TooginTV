What is TooginTV?

TooginTV is a music related variety show that has been live on twitch every Thursday for 4 years. Now on tiktok too!

Musicians spontaneously gather on Thursdays for some Toogin style jamming. We even take some requests. Toogin is an ancient streaming art, Where talented musicians play whatever songs they feel like live to the internet, even if they don't all know how to play the song when they start it.
